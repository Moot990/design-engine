**Pinterest:** group by style/room/teaching point. Compare saves divided by views
and outbound clicks divided by views. Keep repeatable useful concepts. Change
weak hooks or irrelevant destinations. Pause a weak variant after at least 10
distinct Pins and 1,000 combined impressions; below that, record insufficient data.

**Shorts:** compare retention, viewed-vs-swiped, useful comments and channel-profile
traffic, not description clicks. Keep lessons with improving retention. Change
slow openings or repetitive narration. Cut a format after 10 distinct Shorts
with consistently weaker retention than your own channel median. More voices
alone do not make a format original or eligible for ad revenue.

**Digital products:** compare qualified store visits, purchases, conversion and
earnings. Keep themes with demand. Revise a listing/preview after 100 qualified
visits and no sale. Pause a theme with no evidence of demand after two revisions.
Do not produce more near-identical PDFs simply to increase the product count.

**Affiliate:** check earnings per outbound click where attribution exists. Use the
actual Saudi Associates reporting window. Do not infer sales from views or count
the same earnings under multiple posts. A public search link does not prove a
qualifying attributed sale.

**Overall:** keep the combination bringing relevant traffic or sales within your
weekly hour. Cut low-value formats that consume review time. Do not buy traffic,
add subscriptions or introduce other business streams.
